# Privacy Policy

PDC Management is an open-source app that runs entirely inside your own Frappe / ERPNext site.

- **No data leaves your site.** The app has no telemetry, analytics or calls to external services.
- **What it stores:** the cheques you record (party, cheque number and date, bank, amounts, references to invoices and journal entries) and its settings. This data lives in your site's database, under your control, and follows your site's permissions.
- **Email:** the optional daily digest is sent through your site's own outgoing email account, only to users with the role you choose in Cheque Settings.
- **The author** has no access to your site or data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/vinaymishraofficial/pdc_management/issues.
